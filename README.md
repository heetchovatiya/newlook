# New Look Skin & Fitness Clinic

Static website for Dr. Keyur Chovatiya’s clinic in Junagadh, Gujarat.

Website: https://heetchovatiya.github.io/newlook/

## Editing and deployment

Edit `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`, treatment pages in `treatments/`, and images in `assets/`. GitHub Pages publishes the root of the `main` branch after each push. No installation or build step is needed; `.nojekyll` disables Jekyll processing.

To preview locally: `python3 -m http.server 4173` then open http://localhost:4173.

## Appointments

One appointment form collects name, mobile, preferred date/session and optional concern. It validates and reviews details, then prepares a WhatsApp message to +91 6353383626. The visitor must tap Send in WhatsApp and wait for staff confirmation. No booking database, reserved slots, follow-up workflow or reminders.

## Content and assets

Clinic address, phone and patient review excerpts were supplied by the clinic. Google Maps rating is static and dated; live reviews are available through the Maps links. Doctor images are supplied by the owner or sourced from @dr_keyur_chovatiya on Instagram. Clinic logo isolated from the owner’s supplied brand screenshot. Illustrations are local SVGs. Treatment information links to AAD patient guidance; individual assessment determines treatment.

The site uses Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map. Appointment details are not saved by this website. The visitor chooses whether to share them via WhatsApp.
