YOUR CONSULTATION

What to expect.

  1. Describe your concern and how it has changed over time.
  2. The doctor can examine your skin and ask about products, medicines and earlier treatment.
  3. Your consultation will guide any recommended treatment, investigations or next steps.

A LITTLE PREPARATION

Before you visit.

  • Bring earlier prescriptions and a list of products you use.
  • Write down what seems to trigger or improve the concern.
  • Ask how to use any recommended treatment and when to return.
