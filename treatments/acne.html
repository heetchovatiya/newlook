YOUR CONSULTATION

What to expect.

  1. Your consultation starts with your concerns, skin history and the products or medicines you already use.
  2. The doctor can assess the type and severity of your breakouts and discuss suitable options.
  3. Your plan may include skincare changes or prescribed treatment. What is suitable depends on the clinical assessment.

A LITTLE PREPARATION

Before you visit.

  • Bring a list of products, medicines and earlier acne treatments.
  • Ask about expected timelines, possible side effects and when to return.
  • Follow the plan you are given rather than changing or combining treatments on your own.
