A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your visit.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit the form?+

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp request. Please send it in WhatsApp and wait for the clinic to confirm your appointment date and time.

What should I bring to the consultation?+

If available, bring previous prescriptions and a list of products or medicines you currently use. The clinic can advise you on anything else needed when confirming your visit.

Can I contact the clinic directly?+

Yes. Call +91 63533 83626 to ask about appointments, timings and directions.

What are the consultation fees?+

Contact the clinic on +91 63533 83626 for current consultation fees and any treatment costs. A treatment plan and costs should be discussed before you decide to proceed.

What are the clinic hours?+

The Google Maps information lists an afternoon break from 2 pm to 5 pm. Please call the clinic to confirm the full schedule, holiday opening and your appointment time.

How many sessions will I need?+

It depends on your concern, clinical assessment and the treatment discussed. Dr. Keyur will explain a suitable plan and next steps during your consultation. Results and timelines vary.

Can I reschedule or cancel?+

Contact the clinic on WhatsApp or call +91 63533 83626 as soon as possible. The clinic must confirm any new time.

Can I see recent Google reviews?+

Yes. Use “Read all reviews on Google Maps” in the patient stories section. The selected reviews on this website are excerpts; Google Maps shows the latest listing.

Is there step-free access or a lift?+

The clinic is on the 2nd floor. Please call before visiting to check lift access, step-free access or any assistance you may need.

Will treatment start on the first visit?+

Your first visit starts with a consultation. If a procedure is appropriate, the doctor will discuss suitability, preparation, risks and timing. A procedure is not guaranteed on the day of your first appointment.
